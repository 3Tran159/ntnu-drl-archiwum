 Choosing Source and Target Surfaces Before creating a surface meshes for directed meshing, identify the source and target surfaces for each part. Connected parts must share the source surface(s) and the target surface(s). The same volume mesh distribution parameters are applied to all of the connected parts. If the connected parts are of different lengths, then Simcenter STAR-CCM+ makes an estimate with regards to the distribution in the sweeping dimension. The directed meshing process transfers the mesh from source(s) onto the target surface. Therefore, target surfaces cannot contain internal part curves. Note Naming the faces on either side of the interfaces in a stack of parts makes it easier to identify the source and target surfaces for directed meshing. See Meshing a Stack of Parts for additional information.
