 Selecting the Physics Models for the Unsteady Solution Create a physics continuum for the unsteady simulation and select the appropriate turbulence models. To select the physics models: Make a copy of the physics continuum, Continua > Physics Steady , and rename the copy as Continua > Physics Unsteady . For the physics continuum Continua > Physics Unsteady , deactivate the following models: Group Box Model Enabled Models All y+ Wall Treatment Wall Distance SST (Menter) K-Omega K-Omega Turbulence Reynolds-Averaged Navier-Stokes Steady Turbulent Deactivate the Auto-select recommended models option. Select the following models in order: Group Box Model Time Implicit Unsteady Viscous Regime Turbulent Turbulence Detached Eddy Simulation Detached Eddy Simulation SST (Menter) K-Omega Detached Eddy Wall Distance (Selected automatically) K-Omega Wall Treatment All y+ Wall Treatment Click Close . To review the models, open the Physics Unsteady > Models node. Edit the Regions > Body 1 node and set the following property: Property Value Physics Continuum Physics Unsteady Save the simulation.
