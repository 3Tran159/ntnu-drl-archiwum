Wave cases and parameters

H is crest-to-trough height; λ is wavelength. T is the evaluated STAR analytic encounter period for that case, not a measurement of the transported wave. A pending period is not copied from W01.

Shared geometry and speed: water depth 24 m · air 6 m · z = -22 to 8 m · calm waterline z = 2 m · U = 18 m/s · foil submergence 2 m · span 20 mm.

Current sequence: settle C01, save its computed state, then activate W01a. Both adjacent 0.2 s mean CD and CL changes must be below 0.5%. W01a uses a copy of the settled C01 with full H = 0.50 m at the inlet from activation, with no height ramp or global wave reinitialization. H, wavelength and domain remain the same as W01.

How to read the names and recorded periods

W = wave configuration · a = changed startup version · M = mesh identifier · r = technical attempt. A new r number alone does not define a new wave.
