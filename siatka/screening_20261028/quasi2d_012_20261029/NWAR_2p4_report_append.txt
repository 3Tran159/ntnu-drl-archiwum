2.4 — Quasi-2D mesh and runtime screening
A one-cell-thick, constant-section model was built in STAR-CCM+ 2602 Double. The verified 12% thickness and 2.5% camber were retained at chord 1.19 m and submergence 2 m. The 0.02 m span has symmetry boundaries; reference area is 0.0238 m². No physical rescaling was applied. This model excludes finite-span tip effects.

Three meshes contain 37,411, 66,707 and 123,256 cells. All have one spanwise layer and zero negative-volume, unclosed or degenerated cells. Minimum quality is 0.149–0.188 and maximum skewness 46.7–52.6°. A conservative connectivity audit found at least 15 consecutive regular layers over 97.07%, 97.97% and 98.53% of the foil boundary, with deficits near the trailing edge.

Fourteen pilot runs covered 0 and 18 m/s, timestep comparisons and CPU/GPU benchmarks. For 40 identical M03 steps at dt=0.5 ms, measured solver/reporting times were 107.9 s on 8 CPU processes, 75.2 s on 24, and 53.3 s on one GPU. GPU activation was verified. Longer GPU runs reached t=0.2 s at dt=1 ms in 172.3 s (M02) and 280.0 s (M03), excluding launch and final-save overhead.

At t=0.2 s, M03 produced Fx=67.345 N and Fz=950.313 N on the slice: CD=0.017467, CL=0.246476 including buoyancy, or 0.240584 after subtracting nominal buoyancy. Area-mean y+=44.93, range 4.64–255.17. M02 CD=0.028035, 60.5% above M03; M03 CL still rose 3.87% from t=0.16 to 0.20 s. These first-order startup results are not converged or mesh-independent.
