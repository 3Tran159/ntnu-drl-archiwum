What does 0.5 m/s² feel like? The same hull at different z̈ RMS

Each hull heaves at the Godø encounter period (1.94 s), in real time and at true scale (same scale horizontally and vertically, ~4–5 m of hull shown). The Godø wave (amplitude 0.73 m, λ 39 m) passes underneath; the foil on its strut stays submerged and the hull flies above the water. The scale under the passenger (75 kg) shows what they "weigh" at each instant – that is how the force on their feet changes. Sinusoidal motion: amplitude = √2·z̈ RMS / ω², ω = 3.24 rad/s. The phase between wave and hull is illustrative.

Comfort scale of ISO 2631-1 (Annex C, frequency-weighted RMS acceleration). Note: ISO weights frequencies; we quote unweighted z̈ (as NORDFORSK does). Motion sickness: ISO 2631 filter W_f is most sensitive at 0.1–0.3 Hz; our wave gives 0.52 Hz, just above the worst band.
